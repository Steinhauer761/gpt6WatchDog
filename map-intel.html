Global Intelligence Map

Map approximate IP/network geography and live-propagated satellite positions from public orbital data. This is not a precise person tracker or live satellite imagery.

IP / Network Intelligence

IP geolocation can be wrong by many kilometres or more. VPNs, mobile networks and proxies often point to an exit or carrier location instead of a device.

Satellite Tracker

Positions are calculated from public TLE/GP orbital elements and update on this map every few seconds. They are estimates, not camera feeds.

Person / Identifier Mapping

Names, usernames, emails and phone numbers are researched in the Exposure Scanner. The map only accepts public city/country-level clues or approximate IP metadata. It does not infer or display private residential addresses.
