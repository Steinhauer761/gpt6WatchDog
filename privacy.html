Identity Compartment Checklist

  • Use a separate browser profile for investigation work; do not sign into your normal Google, Meta or other personal accounts in that profile.
  • Keep your VPN on before opening the investigation profile, and verify the visible exit IP.
  • For stronger anonymity, use Tor Browser as a separate session rather than stacking random privacy extensions onto your everyday browser.
  • Do not reuse the same username, avatar, recovery email or profile bio between a private investigation identity and your real accounts.
  • Disable location permission for browsers/apps unless you actually need it. Review camera, microphone, Bluetooth and nearby-device permissions too.
  • Strip photo metadata before sharing evidence publicly and crop out unrelated people, addresses and other private information.
  • Avoid opening unknown documents directly on your phone. Preserve them first and analyze copies in an isolated environment.
  • Do not assume Incognito/Private mode provides anonymity; it mainly limits local browsing history.
The biggest anonymity failure is identity mixing: logging into a real account, reusing a personal identifier, or sharing a uniquely identifying file can defeat a VPN or Tor session.
