4G Solar Camera Recovery

Diagnose an owned cellular/solar camera, SD-card recording, power mode and vendor-cloud dependence. This tool does not attack a firewall or bypass authentication. It helps find supported local operation and the exact failure point.

This saves only what you enter here. It does not contact the camera or vendor account.

A 4G-only camera normally communicates through its cellular connection rather than appearing as a device on your home Wi-Fi. If the firmware provides no documented LAN/RTSP/ONVIF mode, WatchDog cannot manufacture a direct stream from the browser. The practical independent path may be SD-card recording plus physical card access.
