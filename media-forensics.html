How WatchDog Should Report Results

Observed fact: directly measurable information such as file hash, resolution, codec, duration, metadata or a visible timestamp.
Forensic signal: something unusual that deserves review, such as a discontinuity or conflicting metadata.
Assessment: a cautious interpretation combining multiple signals and context.
A missing C2PA credential does not mean media is fake. Metadata can be removed. Compression and social-media re-encoding can create artifacts. Deepfake detectors can produce false positives and false negatives.
