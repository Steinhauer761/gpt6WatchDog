2. Evidence Preservation

Hash screenshots, exported chats, PDFs or other files locally before uploading them anywhere. A SHA-256 hash helps show that a file has not changed since you recorded it.

3. Public Intelligence Pivots

These searches use public/indexed sources. Matching usernames, photos or names are leads, not proof that accounts belong to the same person.

4. Scam / Impersonation Indicators

Identity mismatch

Recently created profile, copied bio/photos, inconsistent name/history, or reused images.

Payment pressure

Urgency, irreversible payment methods, off-platform payment requests, unusual wallet or gift-card instructions.

Link / login pressure

Requests to sign in through an unfamiliar domain, verify credentials, install software or bypass normal support channels.

Story inconsistencies

Claims conflict with public history, timestamps, geography, account age or previously published information.

Contact switching

Repeated attempts to move the victim to another app, account, number or encrypted channel.

Impersonation

Copied public photos or branding while contact details, handle spelling or linked domains differ from the legitimate account.

5. Reporting Packet

Avoid publishing home addresses, private phone numbers, family details, or unsupported accusations. Preserve evidence and report through appropriate channels.
