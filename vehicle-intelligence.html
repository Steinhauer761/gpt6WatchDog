VIN Scanner

What you get

Local format and check-digit check, then vehicle specifications from the free U.S. NHTSA vPIC service. Some vehicles or fields may be unavailable. Recalls and owner details are not included.

License Plate Scanner

OCR downloads free language data on first use. It can misread plates; confirm the text yourself.
What it does

Reads visible text locally with OCR, lets you correct it, hashes the original image, and downloads a case observation with the time and jurisdiction. The image stays on your device.

What it does not do

No private owner database lookup, no hidden home-address lookup, and no continuous location-history tracking of a person or vehicle.

Practical limits

OCR works best on a clear, tightly cropped photo and can mistake similar characters. NHTSA data describes a vehicle, not its current registration. This page has no private owner lookup, automatic recall check, plate matching, or vehicle tracking.
