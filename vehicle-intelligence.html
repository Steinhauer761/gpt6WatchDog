VIN Scanner

Worker enrichment

License Plate Scanner

What it does

Hashes the submitted image, records plate text and jurisdiction, prepares OCR/ALPR analysis on the isolated worker, and links the observation into a case.

What it does not do

No private owner database lookup, no hidden home-address lookup, and no continuous location-history tracking of a person or vehicle.

Worker Modules

OCR / Plate Read

Crop enhancement, OCR candidates, confidence values and manual confirmation before saving a plate observation.

VIN Decode

17-character validation plus publicly available manufacturer/vehicle specification decoding and recall references.

Case Correlation

Link vehicle observations to timestamps, user-supplied photos, public listings and other case evidence without asserting ownership from a plate alone.
