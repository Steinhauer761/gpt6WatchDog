5. How to Read Results

Observed

A source contains the exact identifier or related public data.

Corroborated

Multiple independent public signals agree on context.

Unverified

A same-name or same-handle hit needs manual confirmation.

WatchDog does not retrieve passwords, bypass logins, expose private messages, infer private home addresses, or claim that a username hit proves who owns an account.
