3. Website Crawler / Site Intelligence

This section intentionally needs a public website URL. It crawls pages on that one site after your investigation search has found a relevant URL. Same-hostname only, robots.txt respected, private/local network targets blocked, redirects disabled, and page/depth limits enforced.

4. Reference Pivots

Open manual searches when a source blocks automated requests or when you want to verify a lead yourself.

5. How to Read Results

Exact

The exact email, handle, phone digits, domain, phrase or complete name appears in the public result.

Strong

For a multi-part name or alias, all meaningful terms appear together. It still needs manual confirmation.

Filtered

Loose one-word or unrelated matches are discarded instead of being presented as useful leads.

WatchDog does not retrieve passwords, bypass logins, expose private messages, infer private home addresses, or claim that a username hit proves who owns an account.
